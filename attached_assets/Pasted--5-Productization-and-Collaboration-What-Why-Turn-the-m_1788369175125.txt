#5 - Productization and Collaboration

What & Why

Turn the mature editor into a complete creative platform with product previews, durable projects, collaboration, and release-quality performance. This milestone comes last because it relies on stable document, asset, AI, and embroidery models.

Done looks like

Users can switch smoothly between design and realistic product preview modes
Designs can be positioned and scaled on supported apparel, accessories, packaging, and sticker scenes
Signed-in users can create, rename, organize, open, duplicate, and recover cloud projects
Version history records meaningful document checkpoints and supports safe restoration
Sharing and collaboration provide clear permissions and conflict-safe updates
Large documents remain responsive through rendering and state optimizations
Import/export behavior, accessibility, responsive layouts, onboarding, and empty states receive a final cohesive quality pass
Out of scope

Full ecommerce storefront and fulfillment
Manufacturing vendor integrations
Native desktop or mobile applications
Unlimited enterprise administration features
Steps

Product preview engine -- Add supported mockup scenes, artwork placement, transform controls, realistic blending, and animated transitions between design and preview.
Accounts and cloud projects -- Add managed authentication and persistent project metadata, documents, thumbnails, and project-management flows.
Version history -- Store meaningful checkpoints, expose comparisons and restore, and preserve compatibility as document schemas evolve.
Sharing and collaboration -- Add permission-aware sharing, presence and update synchronization, conflict handling, and clear offline/reconnect states.
Performance hardening -- Profile large documents, reduce unnecessary rendering and state propagation, virtualize heavy panels where useful, and keep interaction feedback smooth.
Release-quality pass -- Refine onboarding, responsive collapse behavior, accessibility, keyboard coverage, error recovery, export trust labels, and visual consistency across every mode.
